COTL AI NPCs - Feature Overview
===============================

This character-only product build adds optional AI-backed follower
conversations to Cult of the Lamb, plus the Tournament Ledger tools.

Follower Modes
--------------

Vanilla:
- The follower is left to the base game.
- No AI conversation is attached.

Character:
- The follower can speak as a character through the conversation UI.
- Character awareness can be adjusted per follower while that follower's
  conversation panel is open.
- Character Mode is for personality, lore, current events, and conversation.

Other Systems
-------------

- Invocations: exact player-defined codes that trigger mod-side effects.
- Tournament Ledger: a fight-pit tournament tracker with entrant status,
  match results, champion archive, and current match overlay.
- Internet Access: optional direct-speech web search support.
- Cult About: player-authored cult context used by eligible AI turns.
- Copy Transcript: copies the visible conversation transcript for sharing.

Default Hotkeys
---------------

- F7 while paused: Invocations menu.
- F8 while paused: Tournament Ledger.
- F8 while unpaused: current tournament match overlay.
- F9 while paused: Internet Access panel.

The conversation panel appears from supported follower interaction flows.
