Internet Access Guide
=====================

Internet Access is optional and applies to direct speech turns.

Opening the Panel
-----------------

- Pause the game.
- Press F9.
- Toggle Internet Access on or off.

What It Affects
---------------

- Direct speech can use web search when Internet Access is on and the selected
  AI provider adapter has native web-search support.
- Character replies stay in the in-game speech style even when web search is
  available.
- Invocation receipts do not use web search.
- Search sources are archived outside the in-game reply so the speech bubble
  remains in character and uncluttered.

Provider Support
----------------

Native web-search support is wired for:

- OpenAI
- OpenRouter through the OpenAI-compatible adapter
- Anthropic Claude
- Google Gemini

Generic local OpenAI-compatible servers such as LM Studio and local Ollama
bridges do not have a universal web-search tool. With those providers,
Internet Access is ignored for direct speech until a separate search provider is
added.

When To Use It
--------------

Turn it on when you want richer character responses that can draw on outside
information. Turn it off when you want responses limited to in-game context,
saved lore, traits, and local mod context.
